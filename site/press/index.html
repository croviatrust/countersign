50 words

Crovia Trust is an independent observatory of AI training-data disclosure. Its protocol, TACET, records every hour whether AI models on public registries carry a training-data disclosure, signs what it saw, anchors it in Bitcoin, and issues portable proofs of absence — silence you can verify, without trusting Crovia.

100 words

Crovia Trust is an independent observatory of AI training-data disclosure. Existing transparency logs prove that something happened; Crovia's protocol, TACET, proves that something did not. Every hour it opens an epoch with a public randomness beacon, fetches the model cards of the AI systems under watch, runs a published predicate over the bytes, signs the result and closes the hour with a Bitcoin anchor. A model that never discloses accumulates anchored negative observations — and nothing else. The result is a portable proof, delivered as a Crovia Seal, that anyone can verify offline. Data is CC-BY-4.0; specifications are public domain.

200 words

Crovia Trust is an independent observatory of AI training-data disclosure, based in Italy. It publishes observation facts, never accusations: what a provider disclosed about a model's training data, when, and — its distinctive product — for how long nothing was disclosed at all.

Its core protocol, TACET (Latin: "it is silent"), is a transparency log whose output is a proof of absence over time. Each hour an epoch is opened with a drand randomness round, the public surfaces of the models under watch are fetched, a published predicate is evaluated over the bytes, every observation is signed, and the epoch head is anchored in Bitcoin through OpenTimestamps. Silence never accrues without an anchored negative observation: downtime and gaps count zero. Proofs are sparse-Merkle non-inclusion paths across a contiguous range of epochs, wrapped in an unmodified Crovia Seal (crovia.seal.v1), Crovia's cryptographic receipt format, so any Seal verifier can check them offline.

PNX, a TACET profile, turns the same machinery inward: it lets a company prove that none of its protected assets left an AI agent's run. TACET and Crovia Seal are documented as IETF Internet-Drafts with public conformance vectors and Apache-2.0 reference code; all observation data is CC-BY-4.0 and every weekly snapshot is archived with a DOI.

LACUNAa TACET level-2 silence proof for a model with anchored negative observationsUpper case. A LACUNA is evidence of absence, not a verdict.
Crovia Sealthe receipt format, crovia.seal.v1"Seal" alone is fine after first mention. One format, one draft.
silenceobserved epochs with an anchored negative observationAlways with observed_to: "silent for N observed days, to <date>". Never a wall-clock count.
We never say — and would rather you did not either: grades, scores, rankings or letter marks (retired in September 2026); that a model "hides" or "refuses" anything, because Crovia records absence, not intent; that TACET proves what happened between observations; "certified by Crovia", because proofs are verifiable, not certifications.
