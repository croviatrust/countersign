What you are looking at. This log currently carries Crovia’s normative conformance vectors — a reference chain of Seals from crovia-seal/conformance, not production traffic. That is deliberate: their Ed25519 signatures are real, and your browser verifies every one of them live below (CSC-1 canonicalization + Ed25519, no server in the loop). Production issuer traffic — Causari first — is appended here as issuers onboard. Section 5 of draft-crovia-seal-01 defines the RFC 9162-style log this mirrors.
