Open protocol · Apache-2.0 · local-first

Your AI agent's audit log proves nothing.

Signed, hash-chained agent logs are self-attestation: the operator holds the keys and the storage, so nothing stops a full rewrite-and-re-sign after an incident. TLS had the same flaw until Certificate Transparency added public append-only logs. Agent evidence has no CT.

Countersign is that missing witness. It countersigns the digests of your evidence — receipts, traces, audit packets, any format — into an append-only Merkle log with signed tree heads, and exports proofs anyone can verify offline, with no trust in you.

Sixty seconds, end to end

# independent witness log (keypair + signed genesis)
countersign init --log ./witness-log

# witness every receipt your agent stack already produces
countersign witness --log ./witness-log --jsonl agent_receipts.jsonl

# export a proof for one action; anyone verifies it offline
countersign prove --log ./witness-log --digest 3b0c…855c --out proof.json
countersign verify proof.json --pubkey <witness-key>   # → ok: true

Format-agnostic

Witnesses digests only. Works with any receipts SDK, OpenTelemetry GenAI exports, audit packets, or raw files. Your evidence never leaves your machine.

Offline verification

Proof bundles are self-contained: Merkle inclusion + Ed25519 tree head. Auditors verify with one command and a pinned public key. No API, no account.

Honest threat model

A single witness is a trust anchor, not magic. The spec is open, multi-witness cosigning and Bitcoin anchoring are on the roadmap, and the SECURITY.md says exactly what is and isn't proven.

Why now

EU AI Act Article 12 mandates automatic event recording for high-risk AI; Articles 19/26 mandate retention. Financial and healthcare rules already demand years of audit-grade logs. A log that can be silently rewritten has near-zero evidentiary weight — independent witnessing is the cheapest way to fix that.
